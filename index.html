ATS optimization

An ATS-friendly resume is usually a readable resume. Use standard headings, clear dates, plain job titles, and bullet points that explain real work. Keyword alignment should be honest and should reflect actual experience.

Resume review framework

Strong resumes make role, scope, tools, and results easy to find. Replace broad claims with evidence, simplify the layout, and make the strongest qualifications easy to scan.

Resume Gap Explanation Guide

Resume gaps can feel difficult to explain, but they do not have to dominate the application. The goal is to provide enough context without overexplaining personal details.

Candidates can address gaps through concise date formatting, summaries, recent projects, volunteer work, coursework, or contract experience when relevant. The resume should quickly return attention to current readiness. Career changers and caregivers may benefit from emphasizing refreshed skills and recent evidence.

Explain gaps briefly when needed, then lead the reader back to role-relevant evidence. Clarity is better than avoidance.
